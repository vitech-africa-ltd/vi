## 🏢 About Vitech_Africa Ltd

**Vitech_Africa Ltd** is an African technology and digital solutions company focused on helping businesses, organizations, entrepreneurs and institutions transform their ideas into reliable, modern and scalable digital products.

We combine **software engineering, web technologies, digital transformation, professional design, automation and business-oriented technology solutions** to create products that solve real-world problems.
